ABOUT MINDHABITS

Behavioral Analytics for Habit Formation and Mental Well-Being

MindHabits is a behavioral analytics web application designed to help users explore patterns related to lifestyle, activity, sleep, stress, mood and other behavioral information.

The system uses data analysis and machine-learning techniques to provide behavioral insights, mood predictions and habit recommendations.

GETTING STARTED

How to Use MindHabits

Follow these steps to complete an analysis.

1

Upload Your Dataset

Upload a CSV file containing behavioral, lifestyle or physiological information.

2

Review Your Data

Review wellness summaries, key patterns and optional records on the Data Overview page.

3

Review Mental Well-Being

View mood predictions, stress patterns and important behavioral factors when supported by the uploaded data.

4

View Habit Insights

Review recommendations generated from recurring behavioral patterns.

5

Export Results

Download available processed data, predictions and behavioral insights.
